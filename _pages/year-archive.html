## My Research Focus I mainly focus on the pathogenic mechanism of Mycoplasma pneumoniae infection in children, including: ### 1. Clinical characteristics analysis Collect and analyze clinical data of pediatric patients with Mycoplasma pneumoniae pneumonia, summarize: - Epidemiological characteristics (age: 3-10 years old accounts for 70%, seasonal peak: autumn/winter) - Clinical manifestations (persistent cough > 1 week in 85% cases, fever with average temperature 38.5℃) - Complications (pleural effusion in 15% cases, rare respiratory failure) ### 2. Molecular biology research Study the interaction between Mycoplasma pneumoniae and host cells: 1. Identify P1 adhesion protein as the key factor for host cell attachment 2. NF-κB signaling pathway activation leads to excessive inflammatory response 3. IL-6 and TNF-α levels are significantly elevated in severe cases ### 3. Antibiotic resistance research **Key findings (2020-2025)**: > The resistance rate of Mycoplasma pneumoniae to macrolide antibiotics has reached 85%, mainly caused by A2063G/A2064G point mutations in 23S rRNA gene. ### Collaboration Collaboration is welcome!
